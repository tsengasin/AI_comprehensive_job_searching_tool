# Sample scoring: three jobs for Alex (fictional)

This shows why the toolkit uses **two** scores. Alex's bars: Fit 85, ATS 80.

## 1. CRM Manager, regional beauty e-commerce brand (Singapore) · 🟡 Tailor CV first

| Fit % | ATS Match % |
|---|---|
| **87** | **68** |

- **No caps triggered.** HubSpot, lifecycle journeys, SQL segmentation and dashboarding cover
  the core responsibilities with results.
- **Missing ATS terms:** `customer retention strategy*`, `CRM roadmap*`, `personalisation*`,
  `Klaviyo`, `RFM analysis*` (`*` = Alex does this; the CV does not say it)
- **Why it matters:** a strong-fit job Alex would likely be filtered out of. Tailoring the
  summary and two bullets with true wording lifts ATS to about 82 without adding anything false.

## 2. Senior Lifecycle Manager, B2B SaaS (Remote APAC) · 🟠 Stretch

| Fit % | ATS Match % |
|---|---|
| **72** | **81** |

- **B3:** Required "experience with Braze or Iterable" (Tier 3) → cap 75
- **B4:** "Own the lifecycle strategy and build a team of 3" vs Operator-level evidence → cap 78
- High ATS because the vocabulary overlaps, but the real fit is lower. Apply only with a clear
  story for the gaps.

## 3. Marketing Automation Specialist, insurance (Tokyo) · ⚪ Skip

|---|---|
| **48** | **74** |

- **B1:** Business-level Japanese required (deal-breaker) → cap 50
