**CRM Manager**, Lumière Beauty (regional beauty e-commerce) · Singapore · Hybrid

## About us

Lumière Beauty is a fast-growing, world-class beauty brand selling online across Southeast Asia.
We are an elite team obsessed with our customers.

## The role

You will own our CRM roadmap and customer retention strategy across email, SMS and WhatsApp,
turning first-time buyers into loyal customers.

## What you'll do

- Build and optimise automated lifecycle journeys (welcome, post-purchase, replenishment, win-back)
- Develop segmentation and personalisation using purchase data and RFM analysis
- Plan and run A/B tests on content, timing and offers; report on retention and repeat purchase rate
- Own CRM dashboards and share insights with marketing, e-commerce and product teams
- Work with the data team to keep customer data clean and well governed
- Manage our CRM platform day to day and evaluate new tools

## Requirements

- 3+ years in CRM, lifecycle or retention marketing, ideally in e-commerce or D2C
- Hands-on experience with a marketing automation platform (Klaviyo, HubSpot, Braze or similar)
- Strong SQL and experience building dashboards (Looker Studio, Tableau or similar)
- Experience with customer segmentation, personalisation and A/B testing
- Excellent English; Mandarin is a plus

## Nice to have

- Klaviyo experience
- WhatsApp Business API
- Experience in beauty, fashion or lifestyle brands

Employment pass sponsorship available for the right candidate.
